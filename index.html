About

I am an Oxford-trained researcher, writer and institutional strategist. My work combines research and policy analysis with experience in institutional development, strategic partnerships, fundraising and senior stakeholder relations.

I work particularly on the relationship between institutions and the political, financial and cultural environments in which they operate: how organisations are funded, how incentives shape decision-making, how partnerships are built, and how institutional structures affect the production and transmission of culture.

I was educated at the École Normale Supérieure, Sorbonne and University of Oxford. I began my research career in musicology, specialising in opera, performers and patronage in early modern France. That work led increasingly towards questions of institutions, governance, funding and cultural policy.

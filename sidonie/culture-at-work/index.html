What this map does — and does not — yet cover

The master map follows the principal public, regulatory, funding, institutional and attention systems. It is not yet a claim to have mapped every cultural mechanism. The largest remaining layers are shown explicitly rather than hidden.

Now partly mappedTax incentives

Theatre, orchestra, museums & galleries, film and other creative-industry reliefs form a public-support route outside Grant-in-Aid and Lottery funding.

Next major layerPrivate philanthropy

Foundations, major donors, restricted gifts, endowments and sponsorship need named flow-level evidence.

Next major layerLocal cultural systems

Council-run and commissioned libraries, museums, theatres and trusts vary substantially by place and legal form.

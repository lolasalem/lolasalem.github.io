Culture at Work maps the institutions, funding routes, regulation, governance and private support that shape cultural life. SIDONIE is the interface, not a node in the system.

What is mapped here

This first layer covers the public-policy and funding spine of the English cultural system, together with local government, charity regulation, philanthropy, earned income, education and criticism. It is designed to expand institution by institution: individual museums, theatres, orchestras, funders and foundations can be added without changing the underlying model.

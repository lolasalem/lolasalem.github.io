Charities &
dependence.

Legal charity status and financial independence are different questions. SIDONIE separates the law from the money.

What this page does: charity status tells us the legal form, not who actually pays the bills. SIDONIE therefore separates legal independence from reliance on taxpayer money, National Lottery money, donations and earned income.

Financial dependence ≠ legal status

A charity can receive taxpayer funding, National Lottery money, donations, commercial income or all of them at once. A public grant does not by itself make a charity part of government; equally, legal independence does not tell us how financially dependent it is on one public funder.

What SIDONIE measures

Share of recurring income from public sources · reliance on largest public funder · earned income · philanthropy · public-service contracts · appointment powers · statutory or charter origin · unrestricted reserves relative to expenditure.

What the network can test

The network view is deliberately local rather than showing every person at once. As dates are populated, SIDONIE can test whether two people occupied the same institution at the same time, whether particular organisations repeatedly supply leaders to others, and whether identifiable clusters recur across boards and executive careers. A shared institution is evidence of overlap; it is not evidence that two people knew one another.

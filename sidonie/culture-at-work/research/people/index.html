What this can test

SIDONIE can measure repeated institutional pathways, geographical movement, simultaneous board/executive service and dense recurring clusters. It does not turn proximity into a claim that people knew one another. A clique hypothesis becomes testable only after enough dated roles and appointment relationships have been reconstructed.

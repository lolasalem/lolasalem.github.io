People &
appointments.

Who leads cultural institutions, who appoints them, and how do careers circulate between organisations? A longitudinal evidence layer for testing institutional reproduction rather than assuming it.

Career circulation

This first prosopographical layer records offices and documented career movements. It does not classify anyone as belonging to a social class; it creates the evidence needed to test patterns of institutional circulation.

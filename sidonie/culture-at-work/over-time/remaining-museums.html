What changed
the institution?

Cross-institutional urtext view. This first pass intentionally contains visible gaps where constitutive evidence has not yet been recovered.

1963
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Natural History Museum — British Museum Act 1963

Creates the separate Natural History Museum trustee body and applies statutory collection/care provisions.

Continuity/gap: Later strategic language does not replace these duties.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Natural History Museum — DCMS framework 2025–28

Classifies NHM as central-government organisation, NDPB, exempt charity and Freedoms Body; Secretary of State is Principal Regulator.

Continuity/gap: Framework expressly conveys no legal powers; powers stem from statute.

1983
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Science Museum Group — National Heritage Act 1983

Part II makes the Science Museum Board a body corporate and establishes governance, finance and reporting provisions.

Continuity/gap: Current DCMS controls sit downstream.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Science Museum Group — DCMS framework 2025–28

Specifies contemporary sponsor, finance and accountability relationships.

Continuity/gap: Constitutive authority remains statutory.

1920
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Imperial War Museums — Imperial War Museum Act 1920

Supplies the early statutory basis for the Museum and trustee/government functions.

Continuity/gap: Later amendments/frameworks require separate treatment.

1955
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Imperial War Museums — Imperial War Museum Act 1955

Modifies the post-war statutory settlement.

Continuity/gap: 1920 remains historically upstream.

1934
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Royal Museums Greenwich — National Maritime Museum Act 1934

Establishes the National Maritime Museum and provides for Greenwich land/buildings.

Continuity/gap: Later group branding does not replace statutory origin.

1992
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Wallace Collection — Museums and Galleries Act 1992

Creates the modern Board and duties to maintain, preserve, exhibit and make the collection available for study/research.

Continuity/gap: Earlier bequest/founding restrictions still require urtext reconstruction.

1833
PRIMARY — FOUNDING STATUTE

Sir John Soane’s Museum — Sir John Soane’s Museum Act 1833

Vests Soane's house and collection in trustees on his death under unusually specific arrangements.

Continuity/gap: Modern frameworks are downstream.

1986
PRIMARY — STATUTORY INSTRUMENT

National Museums Liverpool — Merseyside Museums and Galleries Order 1986

Establishes the national trustee Board and transfers nationally important county collections with parliamentary support.

Continuity/gap: Earlier histories of component collections remain separate.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Royal Armouries — DCMS framework 2025–28

Records the modern sponsor/accountability architecture.

Continuity/gap: UPSTREAM GAP — constitutive genealogy still to reconcile.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Museum of the Home — DCMS framework 2025–28

Governs the DCMS relationship and explicitly conveys no legal powers/responsibilities.

Continuity/gap: UPSTREAM GAP — founding/trust instruments still to recover.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Horniman Museum and Gardens — DCMS framework 2025–28

Identifies the Trust as company/registered charity and points upstream to memorandum/articles for powers and purposes.

Continuity/gap: UPSTREAM GAP — memorandum/articles and earlier trust instruments still to retrieve.

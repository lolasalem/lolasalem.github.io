1983
PRIMARY — STATUTE

Royal Armouries — National Heritage Act 1983, ss 17–18

Documented change. Parliament establishes the Board of Trustees of the Armouries and specifies its general functions: maintain/exhibit the national collection; care for, preserve and add to it; make it available for study/research; maintain a record; promote public enjoyment and understanding.

Why it matters. This closes the previous upstream gap. The modern legal purpose is directly recoverable from statute.

RA-C1
1990
PRIMARY — CORPORATE CONSTITUTION (identified through audited accounts)

Museum of the Home — Articles of Association dated 13 February 1990

Documented change. The charitable company was incorporated as The Geffrye Museum Trust. The 2024–25 audited accounts identify Articles dated 13 February 1990, amended in 1999 and July 2019.

Why it matters. The audited accounts establish the constitutional-document genealogy even before every historic version has been clause-compared.

MH-C1
2019
PRIMARY — CORPORATE CONSTITUTION (identified through audited accounts)

Museum of the Home — Amendment to Articles

Documented change. The audited accounts state that the Articles were amended in July 2019 to increase the number of trustees to 14.

Why it matters. A concrete governance change can be distinguished from changes in curatorial rhetoric or branding.

2025
PRIMARY — ADMINISTRATIVE

Museum of the Home — DCMS framework 2025–28

Documented change. The framework classifies the body as central-government organisation, NDPB, registered charity and Freedoms Body, but states its charitable objects are in the Articles.

Why it matters. The framework points upstream rather than constituting the objects itself.

MH-C2
2026
PRIMARY — COMPANIES HOUSE FILING

Museum of the Home — New Articles + company-name change

Documented change. Companies House records a resolution adopting Articles on 17 March 2026 and a change from The Geffrye Museum Trust to The Museum of the Home, effective 22 April 2026; the Memorandum and Articles were filed 24 April.

Why it matters. This creates a precise before/after point for clause-level comparison of legal objects and governance against the name/identity change.

MH-C3
1989
PRIMARY — CHARITY GOVERNING-DOCUMENT RECORD

Horniman Museum and Gardens — Memorandum and Articles incorporated 29 December 1989

Documented change. The Charity Commission records incorporation on 29 December 1989 and publishes the charitable objects: public education through museum collections (notably ethnography, natural history and musical instruments) and provision/management of public parks and public knowledge of the natural world.

Why it matters. This supplies a constitutive baseline independent of present-day mission copy.

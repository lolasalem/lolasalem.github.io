Current legal object · Charity Commission

What is legally recorded now?

The current Charity Commission record describes the object as advancement of education through acquiring, housing, exhibiting, documenting, conserving, restoring and repairing educational objects and collections, particularly concerning the British domestic interior and its uses; promoting debate about the role and importance of home in and for societies; and establishing or maintaining museums, galleries, libraries and other suitable premises for those purposes.

Documented fact

A constitutional event happened in 2026.

New Articles were adopted on 17 March and filed on 24 April, while the company name changed on 22 April. This is stronger evidence than a rebranding announcement.

Open question

Did the legal objects materially change?

Not yet established. SIDONIE will not infer a change merely because new Articles were adopted or because the institution changed its name. The 2021 and 2026 texts must be compared clause by clause.

Why it matters

Legal change ≠ managerial change.

If the objects stayed substantially constant while public-facing institutional purpose shifted, that divergence is itself evidence. If the objects changed, SIDONIE can identify the exact legal breakpoint.

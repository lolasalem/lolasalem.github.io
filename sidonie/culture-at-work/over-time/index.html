How did we
get here?

The present cultural system is an accumulation of legislation, institutional foundations, funding settlements, governance reforms, crises and changes in administrative practice. This view follows those changes without crowding the current-state map.

What counts as a historical event?

Not every anniversary belongs here. SIDONIE records a change when it alters an institution’s purpose, legal basis, governance, appointments, funding model, management regime, estate, practical room for action or the standards by which it is judged. Eventually an institution can be selected—British Museum, Tate, V&A and so on—and its own history will be shown against the system-level changes that affected it. Every publication-ready event will carry primary references, precise dates or date ranges, and an evidence status distinguishing documented fact, institutional claim and SIDONIE analysis.

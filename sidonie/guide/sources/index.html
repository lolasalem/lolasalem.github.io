Sources & partners.

SIDONIE distinguishes between formal partners, financial supporters, and the editorial or discovery sources used to find cultural events. A source relationship does not by itself make an event a SIDONIE recommendation.

Trusted criticism source

Petit Poi / Pierre d’Alancaisez

Used as a screening source for exhibitions. The current rule considers London exhibitions rated four stars or more while they remain open; the rating remains the critic’s judgement, not SIDONIE’s.

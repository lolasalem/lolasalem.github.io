Sources & partners.

SIDONIE distinguishes between formal partners, financial supporters, and the editorial or discovery sources used to find cultural events. A source relationship does not by itself make an event a SIDONIE selection.

Editorial & discovery sources

Discovery source

Continuo Connect

Used to discover London concert and early-music listings. Events found through Continuo are screened separately by SIDONIE; appearing on the source does not itself constitute a SIDONIE selection.

Main partner

Petit Poi / Pierre d’Alancaisez

Used to identify exhibitions for possible inclusion in the Guide. Pierre d’Alancaisez’s rating remains his judgement rather than SIDONIE’s.

Discovery source

The Base

A source for theatre, performance, training and permanent creative projects. SIDONIE can draw both dated programming from The Base and continuing work such as Project 39, its filmed Shakespeare project.

Discovery source

The Critic

A source for criticism and recommendations across the arts, with particular attention to regular cultural columnists and critics, including Robert Thicknesse on opera.

Discovery source

Engelsberg Ideas

A source for substantial reviews of books, art, culture and ideas. Relevant reviews can feed the Guide when they point towards exhibitions, performances, books or other cultural work that SIDONIE wants to surface.

Discovery source

J.J. Charlesworth / ArtReview

A discovery source for contemporary art criticism, exhibitions and artists. SIDONIE uses Charlesworth’s writing as a lead rather than reproducing the review stream wholesale: only work that SIDONIE positively selects is surfaced in the Guide.
